2025 was the first year in quite some time where I released original music. It felt really nice to get that out into the universe so we can start working on new stuff. I'm incredibly proud of that album and if you haven't checked it out yet I'd love for you to give it a spin at one of the links below (or wherever you listen to music.)

We're still in a beautiful death metal renaissance and I am here for it.

## 3.) Cave Sermon - Fragile Wings
![](/images/2026-02-21-my-favorite-records-of-2025/CaveSermon.jpg)
[Youtube Music](https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_n9IntBMQDB4l4Asory4OatQ4kscPlxgz8) | [Bandcamp](https://cavesermon.bandcamp.com/album/fragile-wings)

I think I liked Divine Laughter better. DL had more interesting polyrhythms that aren't really present here until the latter end of the album. That all being said, this is still some of the best dissonant progressive death metal arrangements I've ever heard. Charlie Park did the singing this time, too, and it came out wonderful! I wonder how Divine Laughter would have sounded with his voice on it...

## 2.) Rivers of Nihil - Rivers of Nihil
![](/images/2026-02-21-my-favorite-records-of-2025/RiversOfNihil.jpg)
[Youtube Music](https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kPtiJu7_PAu9S2BL90ppMBefnOSlWBpyY) | [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/5qwj8HdGnmlctKKAzbbi9m?si=-t0IxB6GRJerRRqGlrbt0Q)

This very easily could have been number 1. Gets better on every listen. Every single song on this is an earworm and digs its tendrils in and simply will not let go. I remember the first time I spun this (me already being a fan of the band) thinking to myself after a few tracks just how catchy the melodies were. The best song is House of Light. My favs are Sub Orbital Blues, Evidence, and Despair Church but every single song on this album could stand on it's own. There's so much clean singing melody that I feel like everyone would enjoy this one, even if you're not normally drawn to death metal (or extreme music in general.)

## 1.) This Gift is a Curse - Heir
![](/images/2026-02-21-my-favorite-records-of-2025/ThisGiftIsACurse.jpg)
[Youtube Music](https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_nFvWBvZdYpKa9aSzlTakDBsD18lAR-Ass) | [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/44Hy9Mt7f130Ad4MlivcBd?si=83155db35b604632)

Incredible album. Long songs that are able to fully realize their potential without feeling forced or out of place. Outstanding songwriting and interesting melodies and arrangements. Deranged and wonderful vocal performance. Stunning drumming. So good. Continues to be my favorite of the year so far. The trio of Kingdom into No Sun, nor Moon into Void Bringer is one of the wildest album opening journeys I've experienced in a long time. Just really good blackened death metal, with a touch of hardcore in there. I feel like this band is where I was hoping Tides Cult would end up at. Hands down my favorite album of the year and my most listened to.
